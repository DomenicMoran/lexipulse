## 2. What the service does

LexiPulse is a reading tool. It imports EPUB files, PDF files, text and Markdown files,
text from the clipboard and articles from websites, and displays the text one word at a
time at a fixed position on screen (RSVP with a highlighted recognition point). It also
provides a library, reading progress, bookmarks, statistics, themes, a read-aloud
feature and data export.

Documents are processed and stored locally on your device. There is no user account and
no synchronisation between devices.
