LexiPulse shows text one word at a time at a fixed position on screen. Your eyes stay still instead of jumping back and forth across every line. The technique is called RSVP, Rapid Serial Visual Presentation.

Each word is aligned so that the letter at the Optimal Recognition Point always sits in the same place and is highlighted in colour. Your gaze gets a fixed anchor.

IMPORT
LexiPulse reads your own files.

• EPUB, version 2 and 3, including chapter structure and metadata
• PDF with a smart filter: headers, footers, page numbers and table rows are detected and removed, and words split across a line break are joined again
• Web articles by URL, without menus, banners and comment sections
• Text and Markdown files
• Text pasted straight from the clipboard

READING
• Speed from 100 to 1200 words per minute, freely adjustable
• A dynamic pacing matrix instead of a rigid beat: words longer than 8 characters get 25 percent more time on screen, punctuation gets 75 percent more, and at the end of a paragraph the display time doubles. The rhythm of the text survives.
• Warm-up ramp: after every start the stream begins at 40 percent of your chosen speed and accelerates to the full value, so getting back in is easier
• Monospace typography in JetBrains Mono so every word lands exactly on the recognition point
• 10-word rewind for when your attention slipped
• Jump to the previous or next sentence, paragraph or chapter
• Read-aloud: speech output runs in sync with the RSVP stream

CONTROLS
• Gestures: tap to play and pause, swipe to rewind or move to the next section
• 4 themes: OLED Black, Graphite, Sepia, Minimal White
• 3 accent colours for the recognition point: Coral Red, Neon Amber, Cyber Green
• OLED Black is true black and therefore easy on OLED displays

LIBRARY AND PROGRESS
• Offline library holding every imported document
• Reading progress is remembered per document, so you always continue where you stopped
• Bookmarks anywhere in a text
• Statistics with reading streak, words read and effective words per minute
• Export all of your data as a JSON file at any time, without going through us (Art. 20 GDPR)

PRIVACY
LexiPulse needs no account and no registration. Your documents are processed and stored exclusively on your device. Nothing is uploaded and we have no access to it. There is no tracking, no analytics, no advertising and no advertising identifiers.

The only feature that contacts a server at all is importing a web article by URL. The page is fetched server-side and only the text is returned. The URL is not logged and not stored. If you want to avoid even that, use file or clipboard import, which is fully offline.

Once a document is imported, the app works without an internet connection. On a plane, on the underground, anywhere.

WHO IT IS FOR
Anyone who reads a lot and wants to get through it more calmly: students working through course material and papers, professionals facing long PDFs, readers with a queue of ebooks, and anyone who wants to train their reading habits.

PLEASE NOTE
LexiPulse is a tool for displaying text. It is not a medical device and not a therapy. How well and how fast you take in a text depends on the text, your prior knowledge and your condition on the day. Start around 250 to 300 words per minute and increase in small steps. If your eyes tire, take a break and slow down.
