LexiPulse is two ways of reading in one app. The word stream shows text one word at a time in a fixed spot, so your eyes stay still instead of jumping back and forth across every line. Page mode shows the same document as ordinary flowing text, in the typeface you choose. The reading position is the same in both: switch whenever you like and never lose your place.

IMPORT
• EPUB (2 and 3) and FictionBook, with chapters and metadata
• PDF with a smart filter: headers, footers, page numbers and table rows are removed, words split across a line break are put back together
• Web articles by URL, without menus, banners and comment columns
• Text and Markdown files, Markdown with chapters
• Text straight from the clipboard

WORD STREAM
• 100 to 1200 words per minute, continuously adjustable
• Every word sits on its Optimal Recognition Point, marked in colour
• A pacing matrix instead of a fixed beat: long words get 25 percent more time on screen, punctuation 75 percent, and at the end of a paragraph it doubles
• Warm-up ramp: the stream starts at 40 percent and accelerates to full speed
• 10-word rewind, jump to sentence, paragraph or chapter
• Read aloud, in step with the stream

PAGE MODE
• The whole document as flowing text, not just one chapter
• Four typefaces: Literata, Inter, the system face and OpenDyslexic
• Text size, line spacing, margins and justification all adjustable
• Turn pages with a page number, or scroll continuously
• Auto-scroll at a speed you set
• Tap a word to jump there in the word stream

READING AIDS
• Bionic emphasis: word openings in bold, strength adjustable
• A reading ruler that follows the line you are on
• Six colour filters over the page, for readers with visual stress
• OpenDyslexic ships with the app, nothing is downloaded

SEARCH AND HIGHLIGHTS
• Full-text search with a hit list and chapter
• Word overview: everywhere else that word appears in the document
• Accent-tolerant: "fur" finds "für", "traumen" finds "Träume"
• Highlights in five colours, with notes
• A list of every highlight, one tap jumps to it
• Export highlights as Markdown, grouped by chapter

CONTROLS
• Tap to start and pause, swipe to go back or forward
• 4 themes: OLED Black, Graphite, Sepia, Minimal White. OLED Black saves power on OLED screens
• 3 accent colours for the recognition point

LIBRARY
• Offline library with search, sorting and filters
• Reading progress per document, you always start again where you stopped
• Bookmarks anywhere
• Statistics with a reading streak, words read and effective speed
• A daily goal in words, if you want one
• Back up and move to another device, no account and no server
• Merge two devices: the newer side wins and nothing is duplicated
• Export all your own data as JSON (GDPR Art. 20)

PRIVACY
No account, no registration. Your documents are processed and stored on your device only, nothing is uploaded. No tracking, no analytics, no ads, no advertising identifiers.

The only feature that contacts a server at all is importing a web article by URL. The page is fetched server-side, only the text comes back, and the URL is not logged. If you want to avoid even that, import from a file or the clipboard. After import the app works without an internet connection.

PRICE
4.99 euros once. No subscription, no in-app purchases, no ads, no trial that turns into a bill. Buy once, keep it.

There is also a free web version at lexipulse.de.

WHO IT IS FOR
Anyone who reads a lot: students with course material, professionals with long PDFs, and anyone with a stack of e-books waiting. The reading aids also make it useful for people who find ordinary body text hard going.

PLEASE NOTE
LexiPulse is a tool for displaying text, not a medical device and not a therapy. How well you take a text in depends on the text, on what you already know and on the day. Start at 250 to 300 words per minute and build up slowly.
