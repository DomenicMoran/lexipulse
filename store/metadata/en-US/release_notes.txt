A PDF is a PDF again.

• New "Original" view: the page as it was laid out, with zoom, thumbnails, outline and full-text search
• Highlight, underline, draw, add text boxes and notes
• Fill in forms and flatten them when you are done
• Sign by drawing, typing or photographing your signature
• Rotate, delete, reorder, insert and extract pages
• Redaction that really removes the text
• Open scanned PDFs; several photos become one PDF

All on your device. No file is uploaded.
